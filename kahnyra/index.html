Kahnyra

बिज़नेस, ब्रांड्स और पैसे की सच्ची कहानियाँ।

Kahnyra is a Hindi YouTube Shorts channel that tells short, sourced stories about how brands and businesses were built, why some failed, and how money and economies work. The content is educational only and is not financial or investment advice.

About this application

The Kahnyra application is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the Kahnyra YouTube channel. It uses Google APIs to:

  • upload the channel's own videos to YouTube and manage their titles, descriptions, captions, playlists and comments (YouTube Data API v3);
  • read the channel's own performance statistics (YouTube Analytics API);
  • store and organise the channel's own video files in the owner's Google Drive (Google Drive API).

It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's data. See the Privacy Policy.
